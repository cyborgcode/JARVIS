=====================================================
   J.A.R.V.I.S.  -  HOW TO START  (no technical skills needed)
=====================================================

1) Make sure you EXTRACTED the ZIP first
   (right-click the ZIP  ->  "Extract All"  ->  open the new folder).

2) Get your free AI key (1 minute, no credit card):
   - Go to  https://aistudio.google.com/apikey
   - Sign in with Google, click "Create API key", and copy it.

3) Double-click  start.bat
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" -> "Run anyway".
   - It installs everything by itself (about 2 minutes the first time).
   - When it asks, paste your key (right-click in the black window) and press Enter.

4) The Jarvis screen opens. Click INITIALIZE, then click "Allow" for the microphone.

5) Say:  "Jarvis, show me what you can do."

-----------------------------------------------------
 Next time: just double-click start.bat again (it starts in seconds).
 Emergency stop: push your mouse hard into any corner of the screen.
 Linux: open a terminal in this folder and run  bash start.sh  instead
 (see README.md -> Linux).
 To stop Jarvis: close the black window.
